ITEM 1. Business
Bank of America Corporation is a financial holding company providing a diverse range of banking, investing, asset management, and other financial and risk-management products and services to consumer, small business, middle-market businesses, and large corporations. Segments include Consumer Banking, Global Wealth & Investment Management, Global Banking, and Global Markets.

ITEM 1A. Risk Factors
The Corporation is subject to interest rate volatility, credit risk in commercial real estate and leveraged lending portfolios, macroeconomic slowdown, and stringent capital regulatory requirements including Basel III endgame rules. Heightened competition from fintech lenders and deposit pricing pressures may compress net interest yield.

ITEM 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations
Net income demonstrated solid resilience, supported by robust net interest income, disciplined noninterest expense control, and healthy client asset flows in Wealth Management. Asset quality remained strong with controlled charge-offs. Return on Tangible Common Equity (ROTCE) expanded, and the Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) ratio remained comfortably above regulatory minimums, enabling steady dividend payments and share repurchases.
