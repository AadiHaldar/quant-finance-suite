ITEM 1. Business
Apple Inc. designs, manufactures, and markets smartphones, personal computers, tablets, wearables, and accessories, and sells a variety of related services. The Company's products include iPhone, Mac, iPad, and Wearables, Home and Accessories. iPhone is the Company's line of smartphones based on its iOS operating system. Services revenue includes advertising, AppleCare, cloud services, digital content, and payment services. The Company is focused on expanding its high-margin services ecosystem and delivering robust operating cash flow.

ITEM 1A. Risk Factors
The Company’s business, results of operations, and financial condition can be adversely affected by unfavorable global economic conditions, inflationary pressures, and currency exchange rate volatility. The markets for the Company’s products and services are highly competitive and subject to rapid technological change. The Company is subject to complex and evolving international trade regulations, tariffs, antitrust scrutiny, and privacy laws that could increase operating costs and affect hardware margins.

ITEM 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations
During fiscal 2024, total net sales surpassed expectations, reaching record levels driven by strong demand for iPhone 16 family devices and double-digit growth in Services revenue. Gross margin expanded to 46.2%, reflecting cost efficiencies, favourable component pricing, and an increasingly profitable services mix. Operating income grew significantly year-over-year, supporting substantial share repurchases and regular dividend increases. Free cash flow generation remained exceptionally robust, allowing continued heavy investments in silicon innovation and artificial intelligence infrastructure.
